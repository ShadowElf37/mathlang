[1;4m!animateField_raw[0m

Like [33m!animateField[0m, but writes the raw RGB MXFR frames to stdout instead of
spawning the animator — for piping into a file or another program. Takes no fps
argument (there is no animator to configure).

[1mUsage:[0m
- [33m!animateField_raw T[0m — T: [nx, ny, 3] or [nf, nx, ny, 3]
- [33m!animateField_raw f n[0m — call f(t) for t = 0..n-1, each → [nx, ny, 3]
- [33m!animateField_raw f t0 t1 n[0m — f(t) over linspace(t0, t1, n)
