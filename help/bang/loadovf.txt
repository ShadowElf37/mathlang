[1;4m!loadovf[0m

Load an OVF 2.0 file (OOMMF / mumax3 output) into a tensor variable. Supports
text, binary-4, and binary-8 data. Values are stored in T[x, y(, z), c] order.

Size-1 axes are dropped, so a 2-D vector field (znodes=1, valuedim=3) loads as
[nx, ny, 3] — ready to hand straight to [33m!animateField[0m. A 2-D scalar field
(valuedim=1) loads as [nx, ny] for [33m!animate2D[0m. A 3-D field loads as
[nx, ny, nz, valuedim] — slice a z-plane to view.

[1mUsage:[0m
- [33m!loadovf <var> <file.ovf>[0m

[1mExamples:[0m
[0m> !loadovf m std4_relax.out/std4_relax.ovf
loaded m (shape [128, 32, 3]) from std4_relax.out/std4_relax.ovf
> !animateField m
