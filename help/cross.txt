[1;4mvec.cross[0m

Pointwise 3-vector cross product over the trailing axis. Both arguments have
shape [..., 3] (of matching shape); the result has the same shape, with one
cross product computed per cell. Useful for vector fields (e.g. m × H).

[1mExamples:[0m
[0m> vec.cross([1,0,0], [0,1,0])
result = [0, 0, 1]
> vec.cross([1,2,3], [4,5,6])
result = [-3, 6, -3]
> vec.cross(reshape([1,0,0, 0,1,0],2,1,3), reshape([0,1,0, 0,0,1],2,1,3))
result = [[0,0,1], [1,0,0]]   (one cross per cell)
